Professional Development Training #

Workshops, bootcamps, and courses designed for your team or institution, delivered live or virtually — taught with Harvard Business Impact case studies and simulations (licensed separately per participant). Topics I teach:

  • Project Management — planning, budgeting, risk, and delivery, grounded in PMI practice
  • Design Thinking — the design-sprint framework behind my UCLA Innovation Challenges
  • Lean Startup & Agile — build–measure–learn, MVPs, and iterative delivery
  • Systems Thinking — feedback loops and root causes, so teams fix systems instead of symptoms
  • AI Adoption — hands-on generative AI for real work, from prompts to governed workflows
  • Building Scalable Tech — architecture and platform choices that survive growth
  • How to Pitch — structuring and delivering the pitch that wins buy-in, from executives to investors
  • Visual Leadership — communicating strategy visually so teams align faster
  • Project-Based & Experiential Learning — programs where teams learn by shipping real projects, modeled on Epicenter
  • Train-the-Trainer — adult-learning principles that turn your experts into instructors

The flagship format is the Design Thinking Innovation Challenge — the project-based program I founded at UCLA in 2018, built on the Google Ventures design-thinking framework. It became a 4-unit UCLA Engineering course, and I have run it from UCLA to Los Angeles City College (with LA County and JPL) to the American University of Kurdistan — where it grew from 3 teams to 6, applications quadrupled, and Impact Hub Duhok was born out of the program. In 2024, the EU-funded SKYE Net project with World Vision Armenia brought the same approach to social entrepreneurs through IRIS Business Incubator.
