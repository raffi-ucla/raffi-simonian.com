About Me

An accomplished technology and business professional with an Executive MBA and more than 30 years of experience bringing technology and business solutions to startups and established organizations alike — 65+ large-scale projects managed, 20+ products launched, from enterprise to online.

Today I work at UCLA on strategic engagement and innovation, including UCLA Epicenter, an experiential learning program that puts students on real industry projects. I teach, coach, and build technology programs from Los Angeles to Yerevan.

Experience highlights

  • Program Director, Technology Solutions & UCLA Epicenter
    UCLA · 2022–present

    Leading the Alumni Data Enhancement CRM project covering 700K alumni worldwide; designing quarterly Innovation Challenges with partners including AWS, Harman International, and Slalom; creating student fellowships and the ENGR 171 course.

  • Adjunct Instructor
    UCLA Master of Quantitative Economics · 2023–present

    Teaching Fundamentals of Project Management through project-based, experiential learning with real industry cases.

    Los Angeles Community College EDWE · 2022–2024
