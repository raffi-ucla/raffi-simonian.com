Entrepreneurship10

The essentials I point founders to — programs, frameworks, and guides for starting and growing a venture.

Founder Essentials #

  • Verizon Digital Ready — Free Courses, Coaching & GrantsFree digital-skills courses, one-on-one coaching, and small-business grants from Verizon.digitalready.verizonwireless.com
    How to apply
    1. Use it when: you run a small business and want free training — or a shot at grant funding
    2. Start by: creating a free account and taking the personalized course assessment
    3. Then: complete the courses that unlock grant-application eligibility
    4. Check: you qualify for and submit a grant application round
    5. Be careful: grant rounds have deadlines and course prerequisites — check both before planning on the money
  • How to Sell on Amazon FBA: 9-Step Guide for BeginnersA beginner's nine-step guide to starting an Amazon FBA business — product research through first sale.junglescout.com
    How to apply
    1. Use it when: you are a beginner looking to sell on Amazon FBA
    2. Start by: reading the nine-step guide provided by Jungle Scout
    3. Then: follow each step methodically to set up your business
    4. Check: you successfully complete your first sale on Amazon
    5. Be careful: research and comply with Amazon's selling policies
  • Cloud Innovation CentersAWS's university-based centers solving public-sector challenges with cloud and AI.aws.amazon.com
    How to apply
    1. Use it when: you are a university seeking to tackle public-sector challenges
    2. Start by: reaching out to an AWS Cloud Innovation Center
    3. Then: discuss potential projects and collaboration opportunities
    4. Check: you have a clear project plan and support from AWS
    5. Be careful: ensure your project aligns with the center's mission and resources
  • AWS for StartupsAWS's startup program — up to $100k in cloud credits, technical support, and go-to-market resources for founders.aws.amazon.com
    How to apply
    1. Use it when: you are a startup founder needing cloud resources
    2. Start by: applying for the AWS for Startups program
    3. Then: utilize the cloud credits and support offered
    4. Check: you successfully deploy your applications using AWS services
    5. Be careful: be mindful of the eligibility requirements and application process
  • Strategyzer — Strategy & Growth Tools for Business TeamsBusiness-model and value-proposition tools from Strategyzer.strategyzer.com
    How to apply
    1. Use it when: you need to give structure to a business idea before investing in it
    2. Start by: mapping the idea on the Business Model Canvas
    3. Then: pressure-test the riskiest assumptions with the Value Proposition Canvas
    4. Check: you can state who pays, for what, and why you are the one to deliver it
    5. Be careful: canvases organize thinking — they never replace talking to real customers
  • Claude for StartupsAnthropic's program offering startups credits, resources, and support to build with Claude.claude.com
    How to apply
    1. Use it when: you are a startup looking for AI resources
    2. Start by: applying to the Claude for Startups Program
    3. Then: review the credits and resources available to you
    4. Check: you receive confirmation of your application and support
    5. Be careful: meet any eligibility requirements specified
  • Techstars Entrepreneur's ToolkitTechstars' free library of short startup lessons — idea validation, fundraising, pitching, and growth, drawn from accelerator mentors.toolkit.techstars.com
    How to apply
    1. Use it when: you are starting a venture and want structured guidance without joining an accelerator
    2. Start by: picking the module that matches your current stage
    3. Then: work through one lesson a week and apply it to your venture immediately
    4. Check: each module leaves you with a concrete artifact — a pitch, a model, a plan
    5. Be careful: generic startup advice — adapt it to your market before acting on it
  • Amazon Business — Procurement & Wholesale BuyingAmazon’s purchasing platform for organizations — business pricing, quantity discounts, multi-user accounts, and spend controls.business.amazon.com
    How to apply
    1. Use it when: your business or program buys supplies ad hoc and nobody tracks the spend
    2. Start by: registering a free business account separate from any personal one
    3. Then: set up buying policies, approvals, and your tax-exempt status if eligible
    4. Check: purchasing runs through one account with visible spend reporting
    5. Be careful: convenience invites sprawl — set approval rules before adding buyers
  • Kim Perell — Entrepreneurship CoursesOnline courses from entrepreneur and investor Kim Perell on starting, funding, and growing a business.courses.kimperell.com
    How to apply
    1. Use it when: you want structured, practitioner-taught guidance on launching or growing a venture
    2. Start by: browsing the catalog and picking the course that matches your stage
    3. Then: work through it with your actual business as the case study
    4. Check: each module produces something you use — a pitch, a plan, a launch step
    5. Be careful: paid courses — compare against free resources like the Techstars toolkit before buying
  • CoFoundersLabOnline network for meeting potential co-founders and early startup teammates.cofounderslab.com
    How to apply
    1. Use it when: you have a venture idea and need a co-founder or early teammate
    2. Start by: creating a profile that states your idea, skills, and what you seek
    3. Then: searching and messaging members whose skills complement yours
    4. Check: you have real conversations scheduled with promising matches
    5. Be careful: vet potential partners thoroughly before sharing equity or sensitive plans
