Problems I solve

Thirty years of pattern recognition, organized by the kind of trouble you might be in.

  • Business Analysis

    A business problem that needs to become a technology solution.

    I gather requirements through use cases and user stories, translate between business and engineering, and keep strategy, market research, and customers in the same conversation — so what gets built is what the business actually needs.

  • Program Management

    A project too large, too tangled, or too stuck to run itself.

    More than 65 large-scale projects and 20+ product launches: planning, budgeting, vendor and risk management, and the executive reporting that keeps everyone honest from kickoff to launch.

  • Systems Integration

    Systems that refuse to talk to each other.

    I launched a $3M Salesforce CRM for UCLA Alumni Affairs — developed the business case, secured the funding, and implemented it across 700,000 alumni records — and build the automations that cut invoicing from days to hours.

  • Education & Training

    A team that needs skills that don’t exist yet.

    I design and run tech bootcamps — design thinking, cloud, generative AI — from UCLA and Los Angeles community colleges to Kurdistan, and train staff on IT leadership and business skills.

  • Partnerships & Communities

    A program that needs partners, sponsors, and a community around it.

    I’ve built revenue-generating partnerships and innovation challenges with AWS, Harman International, Slalom, and others — and run the summits, fellowships, and executive events that keep them engaged.

  • Career Counseling

    A career that needs a strategy, not just a résumé.

    I coach students and professionals at every stage — early to late career — across IT, marketing, HR, finance, operations, and health.
