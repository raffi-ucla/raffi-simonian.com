Support & Service · Apple Store — iPhone repair abroad
Observed June 2026
✓ WorksConsistency across borders

A broken iPhone in Europe, fixed like it was home

Mid-vacation in Europe, a dropped iPhone and shattered glass. Walking into a local Apple Store, the whole experience — the booking, the Genius Bar flow, the way the problem was handled — worked exactly as it does in the US, and the phone was replaced within a few hours. No special pleading, no “not your region,” nothing to re-learn. The brand's promise traveled with the customer.

The principle: Consistency is a feature. When a customer can predict your process anywhere in the world, trust compounds — the experience itself becomes the brand.
